A mill built on patience and precision.

At SSR, parboiling is not a shortcut — it is a ritual. Each batch of paddy is soaked, steamed and slowly dried before our modern machinery gently mills it to a clean, luminous finish.

The result: firm, fragrant grains that hold their nutrition, resist breaking and bring consistent quality to every plate.
